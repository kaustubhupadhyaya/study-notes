How to use this page

Read a question, say your answer out loud, then compare it with the mid-level answer and with what a senior adds. Tick the ones you can say cleanly. Drill mode hides the answers until you ask for them.

What separates the two answers

A mid-level answer is correct and complete. A senior answer adds the trade-off, a number, the failure mode and what it costs to operate. In the interview, give the mid-level answer first in one breath, then add one senior point. That shape sounds experienced without overclaiming.
